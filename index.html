Economic Evaluation in Health

A one-day workshop for faculty and senior residents, Wednesday 23 September 2026. The day is about reading and appraising published economic evaluations. Each group of three holds one published Indian evaluation all day and applies each session to it, ending in a report-back in the last session.

Venue: Samvad (Board Room), AIIMS Bhopal, Saket Nagar  ·  Enquiries: hta@aiimsbhopal.edu.in

Where this came from. The five teaching blocks began as a postgraduate course, Introduction to Economic Evaluation in Health, and were adapted here for a faculty audience. The opening, costing and modelling decks, the paper pack, the worksheet and the poll bank were built for this day.
