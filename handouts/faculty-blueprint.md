School of Public Health, with the Regional Resource Centre for HTA
About 25 participants: faculty, senior and junior residents, and research project staff, from mixed

This is the run sheet for facilitators. Everything needed for the day is in this repository, which is
self-contained and works offline. The five teaching blocks came from the postgraduate course
*Introduction to Economic Evaluation in Health* (a separate repository) and were adapted here for a
faculty audience; that course's own blueprint stays with it.

---

## 1. What the day is for

Participants leave able to pick up a published economic evaluation in their own field and say
whether they believe it. The framing is **reading and appraisal**, not justifying a department's
purchases. Cervical cancer stays the worked example in the taught blocks. Participants bring their own
specialty through the paper each group holds all day.

## 2. The mechanic

- **8 groups of 3** (one group of 4 if 25 turn up), formed **by specialty cluster** from the
  department field on the registration form. Two groups per cluster.
- **Mix levels within each group.** Put at least one faculty member or senior resident with the junior
  residents and project staff in every group, so the reading is shared rather than left to whoever is
  most junior. Sort the registration list by designation before you assign tables.
- Each group gets **one paper at registration** (the paper pack), the **appraisal checklist**, and
  its paper's one-page sheet with a prompt for every session.
- In the **last 3 minutes of each session**, groups apply what was just taught to their paper and
  fill in the checklist rows. The facilitator says so; the last slide of each new deck prompts it.
- By **Session 9** the checklist is full. The report-back is in **four pairs, 6 minutes each**.

| Cluster | Groups | Papers |
|---|---|---|
| Oncology and surgery | 1, 2 | Thiagarajan 2026 (SLNB, oral cancer); Guleria 2025 (laparoscopic IPOM) |
| Cardiology and medicine | 3, 4 | Uy 2021 (rheumatic mitral valve surgery); Kaur 2022 (diabetes and hypertension screening) |
| Critical care, anaesthesia, neonatology | 5, 6 | Sharma 2016 (Kangaroo Ward Care); Patel 2015 (ward NIV for COPD) |

If a cluster is under-subscribed, move a group across rather than leave a paper unread; the pairs

### Minute plan for the two hands-on sessions

**Session 4, costing (55 min):** slides 20 minutes, stop at slide 15. Worksheet 30 minutes, same groups,
each group costs an episode from one member's department. Last 5 minutes: three groups give their
total and their answer to question (b), the number they are least sure of. Slide 16 ("And your paper")
is a 30-second reminder for lunch.

**Session 9, report-back (25 min):** four pairs, six minutes each, one minute in hand.

| Pair | Groups | Contrast |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 and 2 | a well-built model with a favourable verdict, against one that concludes "not cost-effective" |
| 2 | 4 and 3 | a conclusion turning on a health-system assumption, against one turning on clinical choices |
| 3 | 5 and 6 | a paper that is not really a cost-effectiveness analysis, against a technically competent one |
| 4 | 7 and 8 | an analysis that ends in a price to negotiate, against one that ends in a cost per DALY |

Two minutes per group: what it compared and concluded; the principle it handles best; the one it
handles worst and whether that changes the conclusion; would you act on it. Then two minutes, led by
the facilitator, on what the contrast shows. **Cut groups off at two minutes.** The facilitator key
(`paper-pack-facilitator.html`) has what each pair should surface.

**What changed in the five adapted decks:** the audience line ("MD Community Medicine") became
"Faculty and senior residents"; cover kickers now carry the session time instead of a block number;
speaker notes and a few slide lines that assumed residents preparing for an exam (dissertation, viva,
PG seat, "students") were rewritten for faculty who supervise work, sit on committees and referee
papers. No teaching content, figure or citation was changed.

All eight decks pass `chk.mjs` with no overflow, no em dashes and no HTTP errors. Every study
cited was checked in September 2026 against its PubMed record (through Europe PMC, or PubMed's own
record page where Europe PMC was rate-limiting), with volume, issue and pages confirmed against
Crossref or the publisher. Worked examples on the
costing and modelling slides are constructed and labelled as such.
